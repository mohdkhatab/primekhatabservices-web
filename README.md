## About the company

Prime Khatab Services is a founder-led, bootstrapped and self-funded software and
media technology company operating since **October 2026** in **Rajouri, Jammu &
Kashmir 185132, India**. It was founded by **Mohd Khatab**.

The company builds production software for businesses and startups that need
dependable mobile apps, websites, and AI solutions without the cost of a full
in-house engineering team.

## Services

- **App Development** — native Android applications, release packaging, Play Store readiness
- **Web Development** — React/TypeScript web applications, product websites, client portals, technical SEO
- **AI Integration & AI Agents** — model integration, grounded retrieval, tool-calling agents with approval checkpoints
- **API Development** — REST APIs, authentication, webhooks, queue-backed background workers
- **AI Dubbing & Voice Solutions** — Hindi localization and voice synthesis pipelines for episodic video
- **Media Tech** — transcoding, subtitle pipelines, and playback-first streaming catalog interfaces

